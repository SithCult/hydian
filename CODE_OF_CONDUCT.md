# Code of Conduct

Be the person you would want to meet at the cantina.

- Be kind and assume good intent. Disagree with ideas, not people.
- No harassment, hate speech, or personal attacks: in issues, pull requests, or anywhere the project is discussed.
- Respect privacy. Do not post other players' character names, positions or logs in issues; redact them.
- Keep it about the project. In-game drama stays in game.

Maintainers may edit, remove or lock content that breaks these rules and may block repeat offenders.
Report problems to **conduct@hydian.org**.
